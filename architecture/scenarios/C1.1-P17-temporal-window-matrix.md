| Observed snapshot | Decision derived | Intervening authoritative change | Delayed application | Current vulnerability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PLACED, no PAYMENT_RECORDED | modify lines | PAYMENT_RECORDED appended | save stale modified Order | later payment fact can be erased |
| PLACED | payment may proceed | Order becomes CANCELLED | save stale paid Order | cancellation can be erased; PLACED resurrected |

## Required interpretation

The first three rows demonstrate temporal/stale-state vulnerability.

The fourth demonstrates an independent domain-policy gap.

Do not conflate them.

## Non-guarantees

Current checkpoint does not guarantee:
- version monotonicity;
- stale-write detection;
- snapshot freshness at effect time;
- serializable decision history;
- modification prohibition after payment;
- payment prohibition after cancellation.
