# C1.1-P14 — Participant Knowledge Matrix

| Moment | Customer request can know | Restaurant request can know | Later reader can know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before reads | customer wants cancellation | restaurant wants acceptance | current authority is whatever repository exposes |
| Both captured PLACED | its captured state is PLACED | its captured state is PLACED | not observing the in-flight decisions |
| After local domain decision | CANCEL is locally legal | ACCEPT is locally legal | still no knowledge unless save occurred |
| After first save | first caller's successor became current at that moment | other caller may still hold stale PLACED | can read first successor if scheduled here |
| After second save | earlier caller may already have returned Accepted | second caller's successor becomes current | sees only last surviving successor |
| After both responses | customer may believe cancellation succeeded | restaurant may believe acceptance succeeded | reads only current last-write-wins state |

## Knowledge limitation

Application-level success currently does not tell an actor whether another mutually exclusive decision was made from the same previous Order version.

## Operational consequence

A later state alone may be insufficient to explain why a participant says a different outcome was acknowledged.

Future observability should preserve enough correlation/version evidence to reconstruct competing decisions.

P14 does not add that observability mechanism yet.
