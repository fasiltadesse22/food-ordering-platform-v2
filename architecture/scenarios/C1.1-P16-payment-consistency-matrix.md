## Important interpretation

Counts are observations from the P16 controlled harness, not a production payment SLA or provider guarantee.

## Non-guarantees

Current checkpoint does not provide:
- payment idempotency;
- payment-provider transaction identity;
- exactly-once charge;
- atomic local+external commit;
- durable reconciliation;
- provider query/recovery;
- refund automation.
