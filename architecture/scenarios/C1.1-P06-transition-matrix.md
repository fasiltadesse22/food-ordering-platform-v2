## Critical interpretation

The table describes the P06 implemented lifecycle model.

It does not claim that all "ILLEGAL" cells are permanent business policy.

For example, cancellation after ACCEPTED is currently unsupported because P05 did not establish the policy and P07/P08 have not yet derived the required guards/reversal semantics.

Do not convert "not currently authorized" into "eternal domain truth."
