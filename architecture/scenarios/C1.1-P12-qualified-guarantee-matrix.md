## Mandatory claim wording template

> **For [specific bounded scenario] on [source version and environment], [implementation path] was exercised by [test/experiment], which observed [exact state/change]. Therefore [qualified inference] holds under [assumptions]. This does not establish [missing failure models/authorities].**

## Review checklist

- Have I identified requirement versus policy document versus implementation?
- Do tests actually assert the claimed outcome and its forbidden alternative?
- Do I know which failure stage was injected and whether the state was already published?
- Does a green test use an in-memory stub, MockMvc, a real database or a real provider?
- Is the claim about one Order representation, a multi-step repository operation, a distributed workflow or production deployment?
- Are numbers described as test counts or actual latency/reliability measurements?
- Do we know the event/message/financial authority being claimed?
- Have I named a counterexample or a missing mechanism?
- Would a skeptical engineer reproduce the result from a pinned commit and CI run?
- Could the claim be falsified by a concurrency interleaving, restart, replay or lost acknowledgment not yet tested?
