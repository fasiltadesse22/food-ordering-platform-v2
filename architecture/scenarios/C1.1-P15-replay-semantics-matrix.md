## Interpretation

The current application has selected effect-convergence/suppression properties.

It does not have a general replay-aware idempotency mechanism.

## Non-conclusions

Do not infer:
- at-most-once execution;
- exactly-once effects;
- durable deduplication;
- replay across process restart;
- bounded idempotency window;
- same-key payload conflict detection;
- external-effect safety.
