## Evidence classification
OBSERVED SOURCE: service and aggregate call paths, type fields and repository ownership in frozen source.
STRUCTURALLY DEMONSTRATED: no Catalog price lookup in inspected PlaceOrderService path; no Restaurant operational query in Order.recordRestaurantAcceptance; no external financial provider in recordPayment implementation.
ASSUMED/HYPOTHETICAL: future catalog price authorization, kitchen readiness, finance settlement, delivery and notification workflows.
NOT GUARANTEED: financial capture, stock reservation, current catalog price, restaurant availability or distributed atomicity.

## Strategic versus tactical boundary check
Order consistency boundary is already justified by status, owned lines and explaining workflow facts. A bounded context is a semantic scope of a coherent model; cannot be inferred just by counting aggregates, tables, packages, or application process boundaries.

## Required questions for further discovery
What current Catalog offering model/policy exists in the business? Can a restaurant publish a price effective at a particular time? What makes an offering orderable? Who is authorized to accept a restaurant order? Does an Order payment milestone require provider capture? Who controls inventory admission? When is 'completed' preparation versus delivery?
