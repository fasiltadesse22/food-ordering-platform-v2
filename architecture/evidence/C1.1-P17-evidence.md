## Requirement being explored

A decision whose correctness depends on authoritative state must not be committed/effected after relevant authority changes without revalidation or conflict detection.

## Structural facts

- Order instances are immutable and can be retained indefinitely by callers;
- OrderRepository.saveCurrent takes only the new Order;
- no expected version/state accompanies saveCurrent;
- InMemoryOrderRepository replaces current authority unconditionally;
- domain guards evaluate the Order instance they are called on, not repository freshness.

## Evidence-backed inference

Immutability protects an individual representation from in-place mutation.

It does not make a retained representation current.

A guard can be logically correct and still make a temporally invalid decision when evaluated against stale state.

## Explicit non-guarantees

- no stale-write detection;
- no optimistic version guarantee;
- no serialization guarantee;
- no monotonic lifecycle/history guarantee under stale replacement;
- no payment prohibition after cancellation;
- no modification prohibition after payment.

## Preserved fragile state

The platform remains deliberately unversioned.

P17's stale snapshot harness is the before-state evidence for later version-aware persistence.
