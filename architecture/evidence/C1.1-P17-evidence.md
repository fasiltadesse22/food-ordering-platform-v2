## Requirement

A decision whose correctness depends on authoritative state must not be committed/effected after relevant authority changes without revalidation or conflict detection.

## Structural facts

- Order is immutable but retained copies remain callable;
- saveCurrent accepts only a replacement Order and no expected version;
- domain guards evaluate the Order instance supplied to them;
- repository performs unconditional current replacement.

## Evidence-backed inference

Immutability prevents in-place mutation but does not provide freshness/currentness.

A correct guard evaluated against stale state cannot guarantee temporal correctness.

A freshness/conflict mechanism must bind or revalidate the decision against authority at commit/effect time if the invariant requires it.

## Explicit non-guarantees

- no stale-write detection;
- no optimistic-version guarantee;
- no serializable decision history;
- no monotonic state/history under stale replacement;
- no modification prohibition after payment;
- no payment prohibition after cancellation.

## Preserved fragile state

The platform remains deliberately unversioned.

P17 provides the before-state evidence for later persistence/version-aware evolution.
