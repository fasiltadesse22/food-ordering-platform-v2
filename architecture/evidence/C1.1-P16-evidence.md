## Requirement

Repeated delivery of one logical payment command must not unintentionally multiply the externally committed financial effect.

Local payment representation must not be treated as proof of external payment truth without an authority/reconciliation mechanism.

## Structural facts

- Order.recordPayment appends PAYMENT_RECORDED with no duplicate guard;
- PAYMENT_RECORDED does not change OrderStatus;
- OrderWorkflowUseCase.recordPayment carries only OrderId;
- there is no logical payment-attempt ID/idempotency key;
- OrderRepository explicitly is not authority for external payment truth.

## Evidence-backed inference

An independently committed external payment effect introduces an atomicity boundary that the local Order object cannot control.

Sequential call ordering can move but not eliminate the inconsistency window.

An exception/timeout after sending a payment request can represent an unknown outcome.

Blind retry is therefore unsafe unless payment-effect identity/replay semantics make it safe.

## Explicit non-guarantees

- no exactly-once charge;
- no at-most-once payment execution;
- no durable payment identity;
- no provider-side idempotency;
- no atomic local/external commit;
- no payment reconciliation;
- no restart-safe recovery;
- no automatic refund/compensation guarantee.

## Preserved fragile state

The project remains deliberately pre-idempotent and has no production external payment mechanism.

The controlled test-only authority is the evidence artifact.
