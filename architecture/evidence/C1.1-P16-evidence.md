## Evidence-backed inference

An independently committed external payment effect introduces an atomicity boundary the local Order object cannot control.

Sequential call ordering can move, but not eliminate, the inconsistency window.

An ambiguous provider response cannot be interpreted as proof that no charge occurred.

## Explicit non-guarantees

- no exactly-once charge;
- no at-most-once payment execution;
- no durable payment identity;
- no provider-side idempotency;
- no atomic local/external commit;
- no payment reconciliation;
- no restart-safe recovery;
- no automatic refund/compensation guarantee.
