## Claim-strength rule

The wording of a claim must never be stronger than:
- its mechanism;
- its executed evidence;
- its scope;
- its assumptions.

## Example correction

Overclaim:

"Order completion is atomic."

Better:

"In the current verified one-process implementation, normal completion constructs the selected lifecycle state and completion occurrence in one immutable invariant-checked Order before one in-memory authority replacement. P11 also demonstrated that splitting those representations into independent writes creates partial-state failure windows. Database, concurrent, durable and distributed atomicity are not established."
