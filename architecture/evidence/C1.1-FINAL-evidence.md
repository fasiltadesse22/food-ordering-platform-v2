## Cluster-level requirements now established

1. A valid Order must satisfy its construction/value/lifecycle-history invariants.
2. Selected operations must obey lifecycle preconditions and contextual actor guards.
3. One logical lifecycle decision must not expose partial state/history.
4. Mutually exclusive decisions from one authoritative version must not both be acknowledged as successful when no legal serial history allows both.
5. Decisions based on stale state must not silently overwrite newer authority.
6. Repeated delivery must be reasoned about at the business-effect boundary.
7. External payment truth cannot be inferred solely from local Order history.
8. Acknowledged Order state must eventually survive application-process replacement.
9. Every correctness claim must state its evidence and scope.
