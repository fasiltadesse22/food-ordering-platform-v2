## Cluster-level requirements now established

1. A valid Order must satisfy construction/value/lifecycle-history invariants.
2. Selected operations must obey lifecycle preconditions and contextual actor guards.
3. One logical lifecycle decision must not expose partial state/history.
4. Mutually exclusive decisions from one authoritative version must not both be acknowledged when no legal serial history allows both.
5. Stale decisions must not silently overwrite newer authority.
6. Repeated delivery must be reasoned about at the business-effect boundary.
7. External payment truth cannot be inferred solely from local Order history.
8. Acknowledged Order state must outlive the replaceable application process.
9. Every correctness claim must state evidence and scope.
