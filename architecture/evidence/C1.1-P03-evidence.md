## Important semantic qualification

OrderPlaced is evidence that the current application decision accepted PlaceOrder and updated the current in-memory repository before returning the accepted result.

It is not evidence of:
- durable storage after crash;
- propagation to another participant;
- distributed atomicity;
- exactly-once execution.

## Preserved fragile state

P03 intentionally retains:
- transient in-memory persistence;
- no DB transaction;
- no concurrency control;
- no idempotency/deduplication;
- no retry;
- no broker;
- no outbox;
- no service split.
