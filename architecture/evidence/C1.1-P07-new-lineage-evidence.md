## Guarantees with boundaries
1. **Precondition denial:** no accepted successor or occurrence from the domain method; current normal app path does not save.
2. **Successful local postcondition:** identity and selected business fields unchanged, exact status and one correctly attributed occurrence.
3. **Rejection classification:** business-denial exception differs from internal contract violation.
4. **Guard mutation evidence:** removal of a guard makes a previously forbidden local transition reachable and is detected by two executable tests.

**Not proven:** real restaurant action origin; physical preparation stopping after cancellation; provider payment capture or refund settlement; optimistic concurrency; serializability of read–guard–save; durable commit; crash recovery; external idempotency; public workflow API authorization.

The action source and postcondition checks are evaluated against the supplied in-memory Order. Another thread can advance that Order between repository read and save. P07 intentionally does not implement locking or relational transactions.
