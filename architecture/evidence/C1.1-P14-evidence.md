## Participant-knowledge finding

After both concurrent calls return:
- customer can believe cancellation succeeded;
- restaurant can believe acceptance succeeded;
- later reader sees only the final last-write-wins Order.

The current Order history is therefore not a durable audit guarantee of every acknowledged competing intent.
