## Specific new observations

- A legal happy sequence progresses PLACED → ACCEPTED → PREPARING → COMPLETED.
- Payment recorded before or after restaurant acceptance remains an orthogonal local milestone.
- Rejected/cancelled fulfillment is terminal for fulfillment; a single refund-request milestone is allowed afterward only with recorded prior payment.
- Exact V8 illegal examples COMPLETED → ACCEPTED, CANCELLED → PREPARING and REJECTED → COMPLETED are rejected.
- Invalid preparation from PLACED yields InvalidOrderTransitionException **without a repository save** under CountingRepository fixture.
- A legal accepted transition stores exactly one same-ID successor in the test fixture.
- The formerly permissive P05 contradictory branch is now denied; P05 frozen original unchanged.

## Explicit limitations

- Tests are sequential and use an in-memory repository or a counting stub; **no concurrency-safe atomicity** over read–decide–save.
- No genuine payment provider, financial capture/refund, identity authentication, Kafka, Saga, outbox, database, durable event store or restart recovery.
- The payment prerequisite before preparation and refund guard are **provisional learning policies**, not validated universal restaurant practices.
- The HTTP controller still exposes placement/read, not public authenticated workflow action endpoints.
- The 39 green tests do not prove every possible state combination, concurrent schedule or external effect.
- Historical P05 run 37738287058 established earlier fragility; do not cite it as verification of the new P06 code.
