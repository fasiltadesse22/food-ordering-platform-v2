## Architectural interpretation

The evidence supports this narrow claim:

> The Place Order application boundary distinguishes the modeled customer actor responsibility from invalid actor contexts and rejects invalid attempts before persistence.

It does **not** support:

> The caller has been securely authenticated.

It also does not justify:

- Customer Service;
- Restaurant Service;
- Payment Service;
- Kafka;
- PostgreSQL;
- security infrastructure.

## Preserved fragilities

P02 intentionally leaves unchanged:

- in-memory transient persistence;
- no database transactions;
- no concurrency control;
- no duplicate-command protection;
- no retry behavior;
- no messaging;
- no cache;
- no service split.
