## Important qualification

A Java exception inside the domain does not automatically mean technical failure.

P09 classifies by semantics at the application boundary:
- known expected rule violation -> business rejection;
- unexpected mechanism/runtime failure -> technical failure.

HTTP 4xx/5xx reflects adapter policy and must not replace the semantic classification.
